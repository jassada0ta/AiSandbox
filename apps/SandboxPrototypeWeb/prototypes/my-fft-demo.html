    A chronicle of the Ashen Banner

    ASHEN BANNER

    The Vale of Corwen, a highland of grass, red badland and cold lakes — 512×512 tiles of it. Five of the Ashen Banner meet the Thornwake raiders at a ford.

    Turns follow Charge Time. Strike from behind, hold the high ground, and mind the casters — their spells land several ticks after they begin.

    How to play

    Charge Time. Every tick each unit gains CT equal to its Speed. At 100 CT it acts. Ending a turn costs 100 CT if you moved and acted, 80 if you did one, 60 if you only waited — so waiting brings your next turn sooner.

    Your turn. Move and Act once each in any order, then Wait and choose which way to face.

    Facing. Attacks from the front can be evaded, from the side half as often, from behind never.

    Height. Jump is the largest step up or down a unit can take. Bows reach one tile further for every two levels of height over the target. Melee cannot reach more than 3 levels up or down. Water cannot be entered.

    Spells charge for several ticks (watch the turn order) and land on a tile: anyone standing in the area when it resolves is hit, so targets can walk out. Spell power scales with the Faith of both caster and target. Fists scale with Brave, and units with Counter strike back at Brave% odds.

    KO. At 0 HP a unit falls. After three of its turns it turns to crystal and is lost. A Chaplain's Revive brings it back.

    City chronicle
    How to rule your city

    A Zeus-style loop: roads carry walkers, walkers carry services, and services make housing evolve. Everything else follows from that.

    1. Lay road (R, drag to paint) from your city centre. Nothing works without a road connection.
    2. Place housing sites next to the road. They start as huts and evolve as walkers reach them.
    3. Feed them. Wheat and onion farms need fertile grassland; a granary stores the harvest and an agora sends a food vendor down the road to the houses.
    4. Water them with a fountain, then raise them with a gymnasium and a theatre. Level 5 housing also wants appeal — gardens, statues, columns, and no smoky industry next door.
    5. Pay for it. A palace plus a bureau of administration on the road collects taxes. Silver mines and a trading post bring hard drachmas.
    6. Keep the gods sweet. Altars and sanctuaries raise favour; sacrifice goods in the Gods advisor. Neglected gods send drought, plague, fire and monsters.
    7. Defend. Forts need armour from a foundry. A hero's hall lets you summon a hero to kill a monster.

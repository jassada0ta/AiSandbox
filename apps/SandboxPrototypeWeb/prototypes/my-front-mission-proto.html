TACTICAL RIG OPERATIONS

IRON LATTICE

A hostile rig squad has dug in on the plains. Take your four rigs in, break every enemy body frame, and keep at least one of yours standing.

  • Each rig has four parts: Body, L-Arm, R-Arm, Legs. Lose the body and the rig is gone; lose an arm and its weapon goes with it; lose the legs and you crawl one tile per turn with no evasion.
  • Weapons come in three classes: Melee, Short and Long. A defender fires back only with a weapon of the same class that reaches.
  • Badland costs 2 move but gives +10 evasion. Water cannot be crossed.

Controls

Click a blue rig to open its command menu, then pick Move or Attack.

You may move then attack. Once a rig attacks, repairs or waits it is done for the turn.

Right-click / Esc cancels, and undoes a move if the rig has not acted yet.

Drag, WASD or arrow keys pan the map; wheel zooms; click the minimap to jump.

Tab jumps to the next ready rig. E ends the phase.

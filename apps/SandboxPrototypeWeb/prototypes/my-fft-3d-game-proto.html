    FFT Tactics 3D

    A Final Fantasy Tactics–style skirmish on a 256×256 isometric world.

    Your 6-unit party (blue) has met a mixed band of humans and monsters (red). Defeat every enemy. Turns come from CT: faster units act more often, and magic needs time to charge.

    Rules
    • CT: every clock tick each unit gains CT equal to its Speed. At 100 it gets a turn.
    • Each turn: one Move and one Act, in either order, then Wait and pick a facing. Ending early refunds CT (Move+Act −100, one −80, neither −60).
    • Height & Jump: a unit can only step up or down as many h as its Jump. Trees, houses and water block movement.
    • Facing: hits from the side or back land more often, so watch which way your units face.
    • Charge time: spells resolve on the tile after a few ticks. Targets can walk out of them, and allies can be caught in them.
    • Magic is scaled by the Faith of both caster and target.
    • Archers shoot further from high ground. Monks may Counter melee attacks (Brave%).
    • At 0 HP a unit is KO. After 3 of its turns it turns to crystal and is gone. Raise brings it back before then.
    • You win when every enemy is down.

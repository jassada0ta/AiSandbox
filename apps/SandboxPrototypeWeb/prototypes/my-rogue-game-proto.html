My Rogue Game

Say, that's not food.

You are Wilson, dropped somewhere in a 256×256 tile wilderness of grassland and badland. Eggs appear near you every 30 seconds. Eat them, or your hunger runs out.

Walk
WASD or the arrow keys — on a touch screen, drag the joystick in the bottom-left corner.
Hunger
Starts at 100, caps at 100, drops 1 point every 30 seconds.
Eggs
One spawns every 30 seconds within 16 tiles of you. Walk over it to eat it: +5 hunger.

Field notes

Prototype controls & rules

Movement
WASD / arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick. Trees, boulders and dead trees block you.
Hunger
100 at the start, hard cap of 100, −1 every 30 seconds. At 0 you keep walking, but slowly, and the world closes in.
Eggs
+5 hunger each. A new one appears every 30 seconds on a walkable tile 3–16 tiles away. The arrow next to the egg count points at the closest one.
The map
256 × 256 isometric tiles, generated from seeded noise: green grassland, dry fringes, dusty badland and rocky flats.

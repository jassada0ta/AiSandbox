    Commands
    • Attack — close-range strike.
    • ARM — gun with limited bullets. Reload refills.
    • Magic — Medium arts, cost MP. Exploit weaknesses.
    • Force — spend FP: Lv1 ability (25), Lv2 Fast Draw (50), Lv3 Guardian (100).
    • Item / Defend / Escape
    How a round works

    Give orders to all four drifters, then everyone acts in speed order. FP rises when you hit and when you get hit.
